# Evidence boundaries and later engineering work

The three November 2026 attachments supplied by the candidate inform this release. They are research inputs, not instructions to represent hypotheses as facts.

- **REVIEW:** individual reports about paywall expectations, learning resources, replay controls and recipe navigation. They are not reproduced incidents, a representative review corpus, or proof of prevalence.
- **OFFICIAL:** the supplied source register lists Satvic Food (`https://linktr.ee/satvic.food`) for free recipes; the Play listing (`https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.satvic.movement&hl=en_IN`); official program pages and marketplace. Source-bound FAQ records use R1/R8/R9/R10/S3/S6. The attachment's “300+” claim is attributed research; the UI does not claim a locally available 300-recipe catalog.
- **SCREENSHOT:** Chocolate Milkshake, Glowing Green Juice and Bread & Sandwiches Workshop appeared in candidate screenshots. This establishes visible names, not current cohort availability, content rights, prices or access. No unsupported Bread & Sandwiches cohort or price was added.
- **HYPOTHESIS:** making free value and course continuation more visible may improve trust and repeat practice. No uplift, conversion or retention result has been measured.
- **PROPOSAL:** the ₹50/₹100/₹200/₹250 experiments, benefits and durations are proposed concepts, never live inventory. No payment or signup event is fabricated.

Sample enrollment rights use an explicitly fictional reference day, 3 November 2026. “Until 10 November 2026, 23:59 IST” and expired access on 2 November refer only to those fixed fixtures, not the real user's account. Current Satvic access terms must come from authenticated authoritative services. The player is a deterministic local timeline; no official video playback is implied.
